# Etsy Search Algorithm & Ranking Factors Guide

This reference explains the primary factors Etsy's search engine (based on semantic vector search + keyword matching) uses to rank listings.

---

## 1. Query Matching (Relevance)
- **Tag & Title Synergy:** When a keyword appears in both the **listing title** and the **13 tags**, Etsy gives it the highest relevance weight.
- **Front-Loading Titles:** Etsy prioritizes the first 30–40 characters of the listing title. The most important high-volume keyword should be placed at the very beginning.
- **Exact Match Advantage:** Exact phrase matches rank significantly higher than broad or scattered word matches.

---

## 2. Listing Quality Score (LQS)
Etsy tracks how buyers interact with a listing:
- **Conversion Rate (Click-through to Sale):** The % of impressions that lead to clicks, and clicks that lead to purchases.
- **Favorite & Cart Velocity:** High adds-to-cart signal purchase intent to the algorithm.
- **Review Recency & Sentiment:** 5-star reviews gained within the last 7 days provide a major temporary ranking boost.

---

## 3. Shop & Customer Experience Score
- **Star Seller Badge:** While not a guaranteed #1 rank, Star Seller listings receive preferential placement in tied keyword scenarios and higher trust badges.
- **Case History & Policy Violations:** Late shipments or open cases drastically suppress overall shop visibility across all listings.
- **Shop Age vs. Recency:** Mature shops have higher baseline authority, but Etsy has a built-in "recency boost" for newly published or renewed listings (valid for 24–48 hours).

---

## 4. Price & Shipping Competitiveness
- **Free Shipping / \$35+ Guarantee:** In the US market, Etsy actively favors listings with free shipping or a free shipping guarantee for orders over \$35.
- **Median Price Deviation:** Listings priced significantly above the 75th percentile of a niche without clear premium differentiation suffer lower conversion rates, lowering their LQS.
